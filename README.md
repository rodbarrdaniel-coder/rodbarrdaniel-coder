# About Me

---

I am Keychy. A passionate, self-taught technology apprentice and developer-in-training, deeply interested in learning AI development.

I am driven by an endless curiosity to understand how systems work under the hood. I strongly believe in the power of independent study. It is not that I dislike the traditional educational system, but I simply refuse to settle for it. I prefer to go further and forge my own path—learning by doing, experimenting, breaking things, and rebuilding them better. I have explored a bit of everything to build a solid foundation, maintaining a strict mindset of continuous improvement, and I am currently adapting my workflow to leverage AI as a daily partner in my development process.

My Learning Journey
My path as an independent learner involves hands-on experimentation across several fields:

Automation & Bots: I build and maintain custom bots integrated with Artificial Intelligence using JavaScript. This is my main playground for understanding backend logic, automation, and APIs.

Artificial Intelligence: I am fascinated by AI and am actively adapting to this new era. I am learning how to leverage external APIs and local models—such as Gemini, Ollama, and DeepSeek—to make my projects smarter and more capable.

Infrastructure & Hardware: I love getting my hands dirty with systems. I am learning by configuring dual-boot environments (like CachyOS and Windows), administering Linux distributions (such as Linux Mint and Kali), and setting up older hardware to act as 24/7 self-hosted servers.

Game & Web Development: I explore the creative side of programming, writing scripts with Luau in Roblox, experimenting with 3D scenes in software like Blender and Godot, and touching on web fundamentals with HTML and CSS.

Tech Stack I am Mastering
Languages: JavaScript, Python, Luau.

Web Fundamentals: HTML, CSS.

Runtimes & Tools: Node.js, Git/GitHub, VS Code, npm/pnpm.

Systems: Linux architecture, custom server hosting, and local AI deployment.

Code is the tool. Constant learning is the path. Evolution is the objective.
